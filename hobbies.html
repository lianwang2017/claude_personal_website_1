My Hobbies & Interests

The things that bring joy and meaning to my life

Life is so much more than work! Here are some of the activities and interests that keep me inspired, energized, and constantly learning new things.

📚

Reading & Learning

I'm an avid reader who loves getting lost in both fiction and non-fiction. There's nothing quite like discovering new perspectives through a great book. Currently diving into philosophy, psychology, and creative writing!

Books Learning
🎵

Music & Singing

Music is a huge part of my life. I love singing acapella pop renditions and curating playlists for every mood. From indie folk to EDM, my taste is eclectic and always evolving.

Music Creative
🍳

Cooking & Baking

There's something magical about transforming simple ingredients into delicious creations. I love experimenting with new recipes, trying international cuisines, and baking treats for friends and family.

Culinary Creative
🌲

Nature & Hiking

Getting outdoors and connecting with nature is essential for my well-being. Whether it's a peaceful walk in the park or an adventurous mountain hike, being in nature helps me reset and recharge.

Outdoors Wellness
🎾

Tennis

Tennis teaches me discipline, strategy, and resilience. Whether it's perfecting my serve or analyzing match tactics, the court is where I clear my mind and challenge myself physically and mentally.

Athletic Competitive
⛳

Golf

I'm a proud beginner golfer who loses more balls than I'd like to admit. But there's something peaceful about being out on the course, and I love the slow process of getting a little better each time.

Athletic Beginner
✈️

Travel

Exploring new places, immersing myself in different cultures, and collecting stories from around the world. Traveling reminds me how big and beautiful the world is — and how much there is left to see.

Adventure Culture
🎨

Arts & Crafts

I love working with my hands and creating things from scratch. Whether it's painting, sketching, or trying new craft projects, the creative process is therapeutic and endlessly rewarding.

Art Handmade
🏺

Pottery

There's something deeply satisfying about shaping clay on a wheel. Pottery has become one of my favorite creative outlets — it forces you to slow down, be present, and embrace imperfection.

Creative Hands-On

Currently Exploring

Diving into the world of personal investing and financial markets. Between my MBA coursework and real-world experience in private equity, I'm learning to think about capital allocation in new ways.

📱 Digital Minimalism

Learning to be more intentional with technology use and creating healthier digital habits. It's a journey of finding balance in our always-connected world.
